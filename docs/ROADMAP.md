## Open questions

- Should two colonies with different genomes ever share a rhizome?
  ("cross-grafting"). Currently **forbidden**.
- At what rhizome richness does a single new ramet outperform the original
  seed model? Early GROVE data suggests about 3,000 nutrient units.
- Can a colony be split into two independent colonies without losing the
  rhizome? (Project *CUTTING*, not started.)
